## 2024-04-19 - Interactive Div to Button Conversion for Toggles
**Learning:** When replacing an interactive `<div>` with a `<button>` for semantic toggles (like status indicators), it's crucial to add `role="switch"` and `aria-checked={boolean}`. Just changing to `<button>` makes it clickable via keyboard, but `role="switch"` communicates the precise binary state to screen readers. Focus styles (`focus-visible:ring-2`) and disabling the button during async updates (`disabled={isPending}`) are also necessary for full accessibility and UX. Decorative elements (like color dots) should have `aria-hidden="true"`.
**Action:** Always use `role="switch"` and `aria-checked` when building toggle buttons. Ensure focus states are visible and buttons are disabled during pending operations.
